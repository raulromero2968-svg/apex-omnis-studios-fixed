## Key Messaging Points

### Opening Hook
- Acknowledge their stress/fear about AI
- Establish credibility as former teacher
- Show empathy for their situation

### Value Proposition
- AI as tool for **control**, not replacement
- Specific benefit: "give you your time back"
- Free consultation (removes risk)
- Case study opportunity (makes them feel special)

### Call to Action
- Clear next step: comment or DM
- Low pressure: "no sales pitch"
- Mutual benefit: you learn, they get help

### Tone
- Conversational, not corporate
- Vulnerable (share your own struggles)
- Helpful, not salesy
- Teacher-to-teacher solidarity

---

## Tips for Posting

1. **Post timing:** Tuesday-Thursday, 6-8 PM (when teachers are home and decompressing)

2. **Engage immediately:** Reply to every comment within first hour to boost visibility

3. **Be vulnerable:** Share a specific story about your own teaching struggles with tech

4. **Follow up:** If someone comments, move to DM quickly with: "Hey! Thanks for commenting. What's your biggest time-suck right now with classroom admin?"

5. **Provide value first:** Even if they don't book a call, give them one actionable tip in the DM

---

## Expected Objections & Responses

**"I don't have budget for this"**
→ "This first consultation is completely free—no strings attached. I'm genuinely just trying to understand what teachers need so I can build the right solutions."

**"I don't trust AI/tech"**
→ "I totally get it—I was the same way! That's exactly why I focus on systems that YOU control. You're the expert, the tech just handles the repetitive stuff."

**"I'm too busy"**
→ "That's exactly the problem I'm trying to solve! The consultation is 30 minutes, and if we can identify even one task to automate, you'll get those 30 minutes back every single week."

**"This sounds too good to be true"**
→ "Fair! I'm not promising magic—just practical automation for repetitive tasks. Think: auto-grading certain assignments, streamlining attendance, organizing student data. The boring stuff that eats your time."

## Success Metrics

- **3-5 comments** from interested teachers
- **1-2 DM conversations** that lead to scheduled calls
- **Positive sentiment** in comments (even if they don't book)
- **Website traffic spike** from the group

---

## Follow-Up Strategy

**After posting:**
1. Reply to every comment within 1 hour
2. Move interested people to DM
3. Schedule calls within 48 hours
4. Send calendar invite + brief prep questions
5. Deliver exceptional value on the call
6. Ask for testimonial/case study permission at the end

**After calls:**
1. Send thank-you email within 24 hours
2. Provide one actionable next step (even if they don't hire you)
3. Ask for introduction to one other teacher who might benefit
4. Follow up in 2 weeks: "How did that tip work out?"

---

## Notes

- **Authenticity is key:** Don't oversell. Be genuinely helpful.
- **Teacher credibility:** Lean into your teaching background—it's your superpower in this group.
- **Community building:** Even if they don't hire you, you're building trust and visibility.
- **Long game:** Some teachers might not be ready now, but they'll remember you when they are.
