## Key Industry Trends for AI Websites

### Design Patterns
1. **Dark Mode with Colorful Backgrounds** - Moving away from white backgrounds, using brand colors prominently
2. **Product Illustrations** - Showcasing actual product UI with static/animated demonstrations
3. **Trust Signals** - Prominent display of customer logos, testimonials from executives/founders
4. **Bold "AI" Messaging** - "AI" mentioned above the fold in bold, snappy headings
5. **Motion Design** - Subtle animations, microinteractions, animated backgrounds

### Content Strategy
1. **Developer Resources** - Documentation, tutorials, integration marketplaces, communities
2. **Interactive Demos** - Try-before-you-buy experiences without conversion
3. **Content-Heavy Approach** - Use cases, industries, product tours, guides, academies
4. **Educational Focus** - Exhaustive information delivery in best formats

### Technical Infrastructure
- **Simple sites**: Webflow
- **Content-heavy sites**: Headless CMS (Contentful, DatoCMS, Sanity)
- **Priority**: Lightning-fast loading speeds

## Top AI Company Website Elements

### Homepage Must-Haves
1. **Hero Section** - Bold headline with "AI" keyword, compelling value proposition
2. **Product Showcase** - Large product UI screenshots/animations
3. **Trust Indicators** - Customer logos, testimonials, G2 ratings, security standards
4. **CTA Strategy** - Free signup or demo request prominently displayed

### Essential Sections
1. **Product/Features** - Detailed product pages with interactive demos
2. **Use Cases** - Industry-specific applications
3. **Pricing** - Transparent pricing with feature comparison tables
4. **Resources** - Blog, documentation, guides, tutorials
5. **About/Team** - Company story and team showcase
6. **Customers/Case Studies** - Success stories and testimonials

### Navigation Patterns
- **Mega Menus** - Well-organized with icons, clear nomenclature
- **Developer-Focused** - Dedicated docs/API sections
- **Product-Led** - Features prominently displayed
