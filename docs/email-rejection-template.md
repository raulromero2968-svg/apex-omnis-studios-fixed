## When to Send

Send this email when:
- Application doesn't align with current capacity
- Project scope is outside expertise area
- Budget/timeline constraints don't match
- Community served isn't a good fit
- You're at capacity and can't take on more work

**Timing:** Send within 3-5 business days of application receipt (matches your timeline promise)

---

## Key Principles

1. **Be Honest, Not Harsh** - Explain why without making them feel bad
2. **Provide Value** - Give actionable resources, not just "no"
3. **Leave Door Open** - Encourage reapplication if circumstances change
4. **Maintain Brand** - Professional, helpful, community-focused tone
5. **No False Hope** - Don't say "maybe later" unless you mean it
