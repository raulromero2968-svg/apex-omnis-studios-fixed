## Key Takeaways for Apex Omnis Tools

### 1. Market Warnings Needed
- **Sealed product speculation** is a trap (Ty Wilson)
- **Sports card market bubble** risk (Ty Wilson's observations)
- **Low-to-mid-end cards** most vulnerable to crashes
- **Consistency** matters more than "big hits"

### 2. Reseller Best Practices
- Always counter offers, never decline (buyer psychology)
- Track shipping method performance (ESE unreliable)
- Consistency is the #1 success factor
- Focus on singles market, not sealed product gambling

### 3. Community Insights
- **Competitive play** is growing (Leonhart founding teams)
- **Content creation** is viable business model (all three monetize)
- **Challenges/entertainment** drive engagement (DPM's success)
- **Charitable work** builds community goodwill (Leonhart's Make-A-Wish)

### 4. Business Models That Work
- YouTube memberships ($1K+/month possible in 5 months)
- Merchandise + card sales (Rare Candy, Stan Store)
- Educational content (Ty's tips, DPM's challenges)
- Competitive play sponsorships (Leonhart's team)
