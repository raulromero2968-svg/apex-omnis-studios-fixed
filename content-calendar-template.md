## 📱 Mobile-Friendly Quick Reference

### Weekly Posting Schedule

**Monday** - Market Insights
- Platform: Twitter/X + LinkedIn
- Content: Price trends, market analysis, "Monday Market Update"
- Format: Static image + short thread

**Tuesday** - Educational Content
- Platform: Instagram + Facebook
- Content: Card grading tips, collection care, TCG history
- Format: Carousel post (3-5 slides)

**Wednesday** - Community Spotlight
- Platform: Twitter/X
- Content: Feature community members, share cool pulls, retweet collectors
- Format: Quote tweets, community engagement

**Thursday** - Product Reviews/Previews
- Platform: LinkedIn + Instagram
- Content: New set reviews, product unboxings, pre-release info
- Format: Video (30-60 seconds)

**Friday** - Event Announcements
- Platform: All platforms
- Content: Upcoming conventions, local events, tournament schedules
- Format: Event graphics + details

**Saturday** - Behind the Scenes
- Platform: Instagram Stories + Twitter
- Content: Your collection, workspace, current projects
- Format: Casual photos/videos

**Sunday** - Engagement Day
- Platform: All platforms
- Content: Polls, questions, community discussions
- Format: Interactive posts

### Platform-Specific Strategy

#### Twitter/X (@TCGAISociety)
**Frequency:** 2-3 times daily
**Best Times:** 9 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM EST
**Content Mix:**
- 40% Market insights & data
- 30% Community engagement & retweets
- 20% Educational threads
- 10% Personal/behind-the-scenes

**Post Types:**
- Short threads (3-5 tweets)
- Quote tweets with commentary
- Polls and questions
- Data visualizations

#### LinkedIn
**Frequency:** 3-4 times weekly
**Best Times:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8 AM - 12 PM EST
**Content Mix:**
- 50% Professional insights (business of collecting, market analysis)
- 30% Educational content (investment strategies, portfolio management)
- 20% Personal brand building (your journey, lessons learned)

**Post Types:**
- Long-form posts (1200-1500 characters)
- Professional graphics
- Case studies
- Industry analysis

#### Instagram
**Frequency:** 4-5 times weekly
**Best Times:** 11 AM, 2 PM, 7 PM EST
**Content Mix:**
- 40% Visual showcases (cards, collections, events)
- 30% Educational carousels
- 20% Behind-the-scenes/personal
- 10% Reels/short videos

**Post Types:**
- High-quality photos
- Carousel posts (educational)
- Reels (15-30 seconds)
- Stories (daily engagement)

#### Facebook
**Frequency:** 3-4 times weekly
**Best Times:** 1 PM - 3 PM EST
**Content Mix:**
- 50% Community-focused content
- 30% Educational posts
- 20% Event announcements

**Post Types:**
- Longer captions with context
- Event posts
- Group discussions
- Photo albums

## 🎯 Content Pillars

### 1. Market Intelligence (30%)
**Goal:** Establish authority in TCG market analysis

**Content Ideas:**
- Weekly price trend reports
- Tournament result impact analysis
- Set release market predictions
- "Card of the Week" deep dives
- Market manipulation warnings (anti-scalper messaging)

**Visuals:** Charts, graphs, price comparisons

---

### 2. Education (30%)
**Goal:** Help collectors make informed decisions

**Content Ideas:**
- Grading guides (PSA, BGS, CGC)
- Storage and preservation tips
- How to spot fakes/counterfeits
- Investment vs. collecting mindset
- Budget-friendly collecting strategies

**Visuals:** Infographics, step-by-step guides, comparison images

---

### 3. Community Building (25%)
**Goal:** Foster authentic collector community

**Content Ideas:**
- Collector spotlights
- "Show your pulls" features
- Community polls and questions
- Local game store shoutouts
- Convention meetup coordination

**Visuals:** User-generated content, community photos

---

### 4. Events & News (15%)
**Goal:** Keep community informed and engaged

**Content Ideas:**
- Convention schedules
- Product release calendars
- Tournament announcements
- Industry news commentary
- Local event promotion

**Visuals:** Event graphics, calendars, announcement cards

## 📊 Content Creation Workflow

### Weekly Routine

**Sunday (Planning Day)**
- Review last week's performance
- Plan next week's content themes
- Schedule major posts
- Prepare content drafts

**Monday-Friday (Creation & Posting)**
- Create 1-2 pieces of new content daily
- Engage with community (30 min/day)
- Monitor mentions and DMs
- Share relevant industry news

**Saturday (Batch Creation)**
- Create next week's graphics in Midjourney
- Generate video content in Runway ML
- Write educational threads
- Prepare carousel posts

## 🚫 Anti-Scalper Messaging

**Integrate into content regularly:**

- "We're here to help collectors enjoy the hobby, not exploit it"
- "Fair access for all - say no to scalpers"
- "Build your collection with passion, not FOMO"
- "Support your local game store, not resellers"
- "Collecting is about community, not profit maximization"

**When discussing market trends:**
- Always emphasize long-term collecting over short-term flipping
- Call out manipulative practices
- Promote fair pricing and ethical selling
- Highlight community-focused vendors
